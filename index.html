Good to know

Questions, answered.

Where do Elaneer & Co tender coconuts come from?

Every coconut is hand-picked from our own farm at Sedapalayam, Cuddalore, Tamil Nadu, and goes straight from the farm to your glass.

Does Elaneer & Co tender coconut water have added ice, sugar or preservatives?

No. It is naturally chilled and never diluted with ice, with no sugar and no preservatives. It is just tender coconut, nothing extra.

Where can I buy Elaneer & Co tender coconut water?

You can find us at Indira Nagar, Neyveli Township, Neyveli (9003083053) and at Jasem Food Street, Kelambakkam, OMR, Chennai (7373222656).

How is the tender coconut water prepared?

The coconut is hand-picked, carefully opened with clean tools and gloves with no grinding, and the water goes straight to the cup and through multi-stage filtration. It is then covered, sealed and served naturally chilled.

What is Elaneer sarbath?

Elaneer sarbath is made with 95% fresh elaneer (tender coconut water) and 5% nannari.
